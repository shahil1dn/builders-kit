## Known-shaky effects to watch (not yet recommended, not yet formally blocked)

These come up constantly in CRO folklore and are widely overstated. Verify hard (or refuse) if tempted:

- **Ego depletion** — willpower-as-finite-resource; large registered replications found ~zero effect. Do not build flows on 'decision fatigue' framing without fresh evidence.
- **Social/behavioural priming** (e.g. money priming, elderly-walking-slow) — flagship replication-crisis casualties.
- **Power posing** — physiological/behavioural claims largely failed replication.
- **Generic 'colour psychology'** (red=urgency, blue=trust as universal laws) — no robust context-free effect; colour works via contrast and brand fit, not innate meaning.
- **Universal 'choice overload'** (Iyengar jam study as a law) — Scheibehenne et al. (2010) meta-analysis found a mean effect near zero; it is moderated, not universal.
